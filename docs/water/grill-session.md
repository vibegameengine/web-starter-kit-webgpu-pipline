# Water grill session (mattpocock grill-me)

Grilling agent asks root-frontier questions; the builder answers with decisions. Each round is appended. Decisions here supersede the knowledge base where they differ.

## Round 1 — root decisions

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|----------|----------|
| Q1 | Target: reference look vs physics | **(c)** Physics as the model, art-directed *parameters* only (wind, swell, absorption, particulate). No non-physical shader terms. Every fudge listed (`envStrength 0.55`, `sunLight = sun·(1.6·up+0.5)`, `+1.5 m` path, `surge` ring) is removed and replaced by the physical term or nothing. Where the reference is physically inconsistent (lace foam on calm water), the swell/wind parameters are chosen so the solver *produces* a comparable foam band; if it cannot, the reference loses. |
| Q2 | Cameras | **(b)** Free orbit, elevation ≥ 10°. No underwater, no Snell's window. Cut face at eye level is in scope (Q15). |
| Q3 | Physics architecture | **(c)** Well-balanced finite-volume SWE (Kurganov–Petrova, central-upwind, hydrostatic reconstruction for wet/dry, positivity-preserving) replaces the virtual-pipe scheme; no flux smoothing. Wind waves: keep the spectral Airy sum *as the physical model* but evaluate it **once per frame into the field texture** (Q11), not per vertex/fragment. FFT is scheduled as the optimisation of that same statistics when the component count limits quality, not before. |
| Q4 | Precision | **(a)** State stores surface deviation `η − level` (plus u, v, foam) in RGBA16F; rest depth `level − b` is a static R32F texture. `d = (η − level) + (level − b)` reconstructed in shader. Readback decodes the same way. |
| Q5 | Time coupling | **(a)** Fixed 2.5 ms sim step with an accumulator; cap the accumulated lag at 100 ms (drop time, never slow the water). Preroll uses the same fixed step. |
| Q6 | Boundary | **(b)** Open faces (−x, +z): flux-forced incident swell with local `k = ω/√(g·d)` + Sommerfeld radiation for the outgoing part + a 0.6 m sponge. Walls (+x, −z) stay reflective (they are the slab cut). Swell spec lives in one place: `WaterSpec` (A, T, direction) exported from `water/spec.ts`, consumed by the sim, the docs and the inspector. |
| Q7 | Dispersion/shoaling | **(a)** 3 Newton steps for `k(h)`; shoaling only where `k·h < π` (Green's law with the finite-depth group-velocity form, not `h^-1/4` blanket); Miche steepness limit `H/λ ≤ 0.14·tanh(kh)` clamps amplitude and feeds a breaking-foam source. |
| Q8 | Compositing | **(a)+(b)** Single-layer overlay stays. Overlay pass seeds its depth attachment from the scene depth so the hardware z-test handles palms/rocks intersections; the manual 3 mm bias goes away. Water writes its own depth. `MeshStandardNodeMaterial` light loop is dropped: the water is a `MeshBasicNodeMaterial`-style unlit node with all shading explicit (the material.color that never contributes goes). |
| Q9 | Medium | **(c)** Pure-water absorption (Pope & Fry: 0.28/0.057/0.009 at 620/550/450 nm) + Jerlov-II particulate term as the *single* art knob (scatter albedo fixed, `σs` scalar). One `σt = σa + σs`. In-scatter lit by `(1−F)·E_sun·exp(−σt·L_sun)` with the actual sun path. Lagoon colour emerges from sand albedo × transmission; no separate scatter colour. |
| Q10 | Refraction | **(b)** Snell refraction of the view ray (n = 1.333), march to the reconstructed floor, reproject, resample once, keep the in-front rejection. |
| Q11 | Budget | **(a)** 60 fps at 1600×900, ≤ 4 ms for all water passes. Structural move: one 1024² field texture per frame `(η, ∂η/∂x, ∂η/∂z, foam)` + one for `(wetness, breaking, caustic intensity, —)`; every consumer (vertex, fragment, caustics, foam, sand) samples the field, never the component sum. |
| Q12 | Foam | **(b)** Physical sources only, all written into the persistent field by the sim: breaking (Miche limit exceeded / `H/h > 0.78`), Froude > 1 transitions (bore fronts), convergence `∇·u < −threshold`, run-up front. Foam is shaded as a lit diffuse layer (albedo 0.9, receives sun shadow + sky, coverage-based), thins with age. Noise only as sub-cell detail of the physical mask. Mesh resolution ≥ sim resolution (512² mesh for 512² sim); dry cells clamp to the bathymetry so the film fades on the sand — no `level − 0.15` wall. |
| Q13 | Caustics | **(c)** Water pass computes a caustic map from the field normals (refract sun, `1/|det(I + h·∇r)|`, sun-occluded, depth-blurred) into a texture; sand and rock shaders sample it and apply it inside their own lighting (so shadowed sand does not brighten). |
| Q14 | Reflections | **(b)** SSR against the beauty + depth (already bound) with sky fallback; sun glint via GGX with the sun's 0.53° angular radius and roughness widened by the analytic slope variance of the wind spectrum (Toksvig from the same components). Noise glitter removed. |
| Q15 | Cut faces | **(b)** First class: real path length to the sand wall via depth (no `+1.5`), caustic map on the wall, Fresnel at eye-level angles, foam line at the waterline. |


## Round 2 — implications of Round 1

Numbers computed by the grill from the code; decisions below supersede Round 1 where noted.

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|----------|----------|
| Q16 | Swell is not a shallow-water wave at T = 1.4 s | **(a)** Swell period T = 3.2 s, amplitude chosen by Q20 so the breaking band lands where the reference has lace. `WaterSpec` asserts `kh(d_max) ≤ 1` at construction. Declared ceiling of the whole system: non-dispersive swell, no plunging breakers, no bubble column; wave *texture* is the Airy field. |
| Q17 | 2.5 ms × 512² × KP × 100 ms cap over-determined | **(b)** now: 384² grid (3.1 cm), Δt = 2.0 ms fixed, ≤ 9 substeps per frame, time beyond that is **dropped** (never stretched, never slowed). Supersedes Q5's 2.5 ms / 100 ms lag cap. (c) fused compute stages when the compute path lands. One `_gputime.mjs` reading of the current 16 draws is taken when ⑥ lands, to confirm (b) fits ≤ 1.5 ms of the 4 ms budget. |
| Q18 | KP state, reconstruction, dry threshold, bathymetry | **(c) + minmod θ = 1.3**. State = (η − level, hu, hv, —) RGBA16F; h reconstructed in fp32 from a static R32F rest-depth texture at sim resolution (`field.toTexture(384)`), so b is exact at cell centres and linear across faces. Desingularised u = √2·h·hu/√(h⁴ + max(h⁴, ε)), ε = Δx⁴. Dry threshold ε_dry = 2.5e-4 m for the solver; the 3 mm render film threshold stays separate. Supersedes Q4's primitive (u, v). Acceptance: lake at rest holds |η − level| < 1e-4 m for 10 s with the swell off. |
| Q19 | Boundary: three mechanisms, sponge too short | **(a)** Characteristic boundary on −x and +z: impose the incoming Riemann invariant u + 2√(gd) from `WaterSpec`, let u − 2√(gd) leave. No sponge, no Sommerfeld, no relaxation strip. Supersedes Q6. Acceptance: mean η over the slab drifts < 1 mm over 60 s; ×25 section shows no standing-wave nodes along −x after 30 s. |
| Q20 | Breaking criterion | **(c) + run-up front**: foam source = hydraulic-jump dissipation from KP shocks, D = g·√(g·h̄)·(Δh)³/(4·h₁·h₂), S = D/D_ref with D_ref such that a Δh = h₁ = 0.1 m bore fills coverage in 0.3 s; plus the run-up front (|u| where d < 1 cm). Envelope tracking and slope proxy dropped. Swell A is chosen so d_b = H/0.78 puts the band where the reference has it and is written into `WaterSpec`. |
| Q21 | Foam field: max source, frame-rate blur, no thickness | **(c)** f is thickness: f ← advect(f)·e^{−dt/τ} + S·dt, τ = 3.85 s (Monahan), no explicit diffusion (bilinear backtrace supplies it), coverage = 1 − exp(−f/k), noise only modulates k inside the mask. Age = thickness, no extra channel. Acceptance: a 1 s source pulse leaves a patch whose 1/e width at 4 s is within 20 % between 60 Hz and 30 Hz. |
| Q22 | Foam/sky lighting vs unlit material | **(b)** Unlit node material + explicit TSL `shadow(sunLight)` node (r182 exports `shadow`; if it does not compile for the overlay pass, fall back to (a) with the (1−F) weight recomputed by hand and note it here) + `pmremTexture(env)` for foam sky irradiance, Fresnel sky and SSR fallback + own GGX. All Round-1-banned sun/sky fudges removed. Acceptance: sunlit foam / sunlit dry sand luminance ≈ 1.3 ± 0.2; foam under a palm shadow ≈ the shadowed-sand ratio. |
| Q23 | Field layout, order, depth seeding | **(a) + (i)**. P (ping-pong RGBA16F 1024²) = (foamThickness, wetSaturation, —, —); D (single RGBA16F 1024²) = (η − level, ∂η/∂x, ∂η/∂z, breakingSource); C (single R16F 1024²) = caustic. Order per frame: sim substeps → D (sim η + Airy) → P (advect by sim u,v; sources from D.w; wetness from sim d) → C (from D slopes) → scene pass → composite → overlay. Airy k_i(h) from a 48×64 LUT. Depth seeding: a full-screen quad on `Layer.Overlay`, `renderOrder −1`, `colorWrite = false`, `depthNode = texture(sceneDepth).x`; the manual 3 mm bias goes. Acceptance: no 1-px halo around a waterline rock at 1600×900. |
| Q24 | Spectrum split field vs roughness | **(a)** Field takes components with λ ≥ 4 field texels (4.7 cm); α_eff² = α₀² + σ²_tail, σ²_tail = CoxMunk(U) − Σ_resolved (a·k)²/2, computed on the CPU per wind setting (≈ 0.11–0.13). `roughness 0.07` and the glitter noise go. Acceptance: sun highlight width in the frame ≈ 2·α_eff·distance. |
| Q25 | Medium: Jerlov-II is greener, (1−F)/n² missing | **(b)** Pope–Fry absorption + spectrally flat particulate σs ∈ [0.05, 0.3] m⁻¹ (the single knob), ω₀ = 0.95, HG g = 0.85; closed-form single scatter along the refracted ray with the sun's (1−F_s) entry; transmitted floor radiance × (1−F_v)/n². Supersedes Q9's Jerlov-II. Acceptance: floor pixel at d = 1 m in sun has B/G ≥ 0.9 and luminance ≤ 0.6 × adjacent dry sand. |
| Q26 | Snell first, path 3.9× too long | **(a)**: refract the view ray, intersect the plane of the first depth sample, reproject, resample depth once, one more iteration; keep in-front rejection. Fall back to (b) 8+1 screen march only where the resampled floor differs from the plane hit by > 10 cm. Acceptance: `?waterDebug=path` at 10° elevation shows ≤ 1.6 m over a 1 m floor. |
| Q27 | Caustic map: gather/scatter, det singularity, occlusion | **(a)** Gather at 1024², one fixed-point iteration for the inverse map, clamp 1/|det| ≤ 8, blur radius ∝ d·α_eff, no shadow read in the map; the map multiplies the sand/rock **direct** term (so shadow × caustic = 0 in shade). Emissive caustics removed on both sides. Acceptance: zero caustic under a palm shadow; 2–4 cm filaments at d = 0.3 m. |
| Q28 | SSR lobe and diving rays | **(b)** Half-res SSR; hit colour from a composite mip at mip = log₂(2·α·hitDist_px); reject hits with y < level; fallback `pmremTexture(env, r, α_eff)`. Acceptance: a palm trunk reflected at 5 m has a soft edge ≥ 40 px; no floor colour in any reflection. |
| Q29 | Wet sand drainage and gloss | **(b)** Saturation s: s = 1 where y < level + 0.05 (capillary fringe); else s ← max(standing, s·e^{−dt/90}); albedo × (1 − 0.42·s); roughness 0.9 → 0.75 when damp, 0.45 only under a film. The 28 s exponential goes. Acceptance: 2 min after the swell is switched off the dark band ends at level + 5 cm and the upper swash band has faded. |
| Q30 | Landing order | **(a)** shading-first: ① field bake D + mesh/fragment read D (pixel-identical, fps up) → ② Snell + path + (1−F)/n² → ③ medium parameters → ④ depth seed + unlit material → ⑤ caustic map → ⑥ KP + characteristic boundary + fixed step → ⑦ foam sources/field → ⑧ SSR + α_eff → ⑨ wet sand. Each step verified in a frame (①–⑤, ⑧, ⑨) or in the inspector with the stated acceptance (⑥, ⑦). |

## Round 3 — implementation frontier

Preliminary corrections accepted: the run-up `ceiling` clips and the dry-cell `min(b, level+0.02)` are transitional clamps that go with KP; the film gate must use the **unrefracted** depth sample; Manning becomes semi-implicit under KP with no linear friction; the generator is replaced by Q36; the wind clock must be the sim clock.
